CREDIT GUIDE5 min read

Understanding CIBIL Score Impact on Loan Interest Rates

Learn how a 750+ CIBIL score can support access to lower interest rates and faster loan decisions.

Read Article →

Your CIBIL score helps lenders assess repayment history. A score of 750 or above is often viewed favourably, but approval and pricing also depend on income, existing debt and the lender’s policy.

Pay EMIs and card bills on time, keep credit utilisation low and review your credit report for errors. Compare the annual percentage rate, processing fees and total repayment before choosing a loan.

BUSINESS FINANCE8 min read

How Working Capital Facilities Boost Business Cash Flow

A practical guide for SMEs on structuring revolving overdraft lines to support day-to-day operations.

Read Article →

Working capital bridges the gap between paying suppliers and receiving customer payments. An overdraft or cash credit facility can help cover inventory, wages and seasonal expenses within an approved limit.

Estimate your cash flow cycle before borrowing. Compare interest calculation methods, renewal fees, collateral requirements and repayment conditions. Use short-term facilities for operating needs and review the balance regularly.

SECURED VS UNSECURED6 min read

Gold Loan vs Personal Loan: Which Credit Option Suits You?

Compare interest rates, collateral safety, LTV ratios and repayment flexibility.

Read Article →

A gold loan is secured against pledged gold; the eligible amount depends on its valuation and the lender’s applicable loan-to-value limits. A personal loan is typically unsecured and assessed using income and credit history.

Compare the total cost, tenure and repayment schedule. With a gold loan, missed repayments can put the pledged gold at risk under the agreement. Both products can affect your credit history. Read all charges and default terms before signing.

TAX ADVISORY7 min read

Tax Exemption Benefits Under Home Loans (Section 24 & 80C)

Understand potential income tax deductions on principal and interest repayments each financial year.

Read Article →

Under the old tax regime, eligible home loan principal repayments may qualify within the overall Section 80C limit of ₹1.5 lakh. Eligible interest on a self-occupied home may qualify for a deduction up to ₹2 lakh under Section 24(b), subject to conditions.

Eligibility depends on the property, completion timelines, tax regime and current law. The new tax regime generally does not allow these deductions for a self-occupied home. Check the latest Income Tax Department guidance or consult a qualified tax adviser for your circumstances.

These guides provide general information. Loan eligibility, rates and tax benefits depend on your circumstances and current rules.

⚠️ CRITICAL ADVISORY

What Happens If You Don't Cancel Your Loan?

If an approved or active loan application is not canceled on time through our official portal, it leads to severe financial, credit, and administrative impacts.

Impact 1

Unwanted EMI & Interest Charges

Active loans trigger scheduled monthly EMIs. Without cancellation, interest compounds daily, increasing your overall liability unnecessarily.

❌ Result: Extra monetary outflows & hidden fees.
Impact 2

Severe CIBIL Score Degradation

Unpaid active loans or overdue installments are reported directly to credit bureaus (CIBIL/Experian), dropping your credit score significantly.

⚠️ Result: Rejection for future home or auto loans.
Impact 3

NBFC Recovery & Legal Actions

Failing to service disbursed loans or clear outstanding dues invites official reminder notices, collection agent calls, and legal proceedings.

🚨 Result: Formal collection alerts & legal hassle.
Protection for everyday life

Understand the insurance options for your family, your health and the assets you work hard to build.

01 / Life insurance

Care that looks ahead

Life insurance can provide financial support to your nominees if you pass away during the policy term. Consider your household expenses, outstanding loans and long-term family needs when choosing cover.

  • Compare the sum assured and policy term.
  • Check exclusions and keep nominee details current.
02 / Health insurance

Make room for better care

Health insurance can help cover eligible hospitalisation and treatment expenses. Benefits vary by policy, so review the terms before choosing a plan.

  • Check network hospitals and cashless treatment rules.
  • Review waiting periods, co-payments and room rent limits.
03 / Property & business insurance

Protect what you have built

Property and business policies can cover specified risks affecting buildings, stock and equipment. Choose protection that matches the assets you own and the risks your business faces.

  • Confirm which risks and assets are covered.
  • Compare deductibles, exclusions and claim requirements.

Coverage, premiums and claims are subject to the insurer’s policy wording, eligibility and underwriting. Read the policy documents carefully before purchasing.
